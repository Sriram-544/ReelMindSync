Install the Chrome Extension

To bypass Instagram's strict rate limits, ReelMind uses a simple Chrome extension to sync your saved reels directly from your browser.

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions/
  2. Turn on Developer Mode (top right)
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder in the ReelMind directory
  4. Go to Instagram Saved Reels in your browser
  5. Click the ReelMind extension icon and click "Sync"
